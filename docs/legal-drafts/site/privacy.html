This is a plain description of what we do with personal data in Liftelo. Liftelo is an elevator maintenance app for business customers.

1. Who we are

Liftelo is provided by [[TVRTKA: full legal name]] ([[OBLIK: legal form]]), [[ADRESA: registered address]], OIB [[OIB: OIB (tax ID)]]. Privacy contact: [[EMAIL_PRIVATNOST: privacy e-mail]]. Data protection officer: [[DPO: data protection officer appointed: yes/no]].

2. Two roles: controller and processor

Your company's data in the app. A company that uses Liftelo (our customer) enters locations, elevators, visits (RMS), interventions, work orders, projects, vehicles, technician names, photos and signatures. For that data the company is the controller and we are the processor, acting only on the company's instructions and under the Data Processing Agreement. If you are a technician or client of a company that uses Liftelo and want to exercise your rights, contact that company; if you contact us, we will pass your request on to the company.

Our own data. For the data in section 3 (account, billing, security, contact) we are the controller.

4. Who receives data

We use suppliers (subprocessors) for hosting, billing, e-mail, sign-in and maps. The list is on the Subprocessors page. We do not sell data.

Some suppliers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case we rely on the safeguards they offer ([[PRIJENOSI: transfer mechanism outside the EEA]]). Our servers are hosted by [[IAAS: IaaS provider under Cloudways]], region [[REGIJA: server region/country]].

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and company data: for the duration of the contract, then [[ROK_IZVOZ: days to export after termination]] days for export, then deletion.
  • Invoice and billing data: as long as the law requires ([[ROK_RACUNI: accounting records retention]]).
  • IP addresses in the security log: [[ROK_IP: IP address retention period]].
  • Backups: disappear by rotation within [[ROK_BACKUP: backup deletion period]].
  • Service records (company data): decided by the company as controller; legal retention period for elevator maintenance records: [[ROK_SERVIS: legal retention period for service records]].

6. Erasure and historical service records

Service records (visits, interventions) in Liftelo are append-only by design, because they serve as proof that maintenance was done. Therefore:

  • we do not delete an individual (e.g. a technician who left) from records; at the company's request we anonymise their data (name and e-mail replaced by a label, account deactivated, password erased) and keep the service record itself;
  • when a company stops using Liftelo it can export its data, and we delete it after the deadline (unless the law requires retention).

Status: tools for a full company data export and for user anonymisation are under development. Until then we do export and anonymisation manually on written request from the company's administrator.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. For data in section 3 write to [[EMAIL_PRIVATNOST: privacy e-mail]]; we reply within one month. For data entered by a company, contact that company. You may complain to the supervisory authority: the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), azop.hr.

8. Security

What we do to protect data is described on the Security page. In case of a data breach we notify customers and the supervisory authority within the deadlines set by law.

9. Children and consumers

Liftelo is intended only for business users (companies, sole traders and other business entities), not for consumers or persons under 18.

10. Changes

We may change this policy. The effective date and version are at the top of the page; we will inform users of material changes.
