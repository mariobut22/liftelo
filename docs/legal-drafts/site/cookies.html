In short: we only use what is necessary for you to sign in and for the app to remember your language. No analytics, ads or tracking, so there is no consent banner.

Public website (liftelo.app)

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Fonts load from our own server, with no external resources.

Google Maps

When you use a map or address suggestions in the app, your browser loads a script from Google's servers (maps.googleapis.com), so Google sees your IP address. We have not verified whether Google sets its own cookie or storage there; if we establish that, we will list it here. See Subprocessors.

Sign-in with Google and Apple

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple you are redirected to their pages, where their rules apply.

Payment

The payment page is shown by Stripe and is outside the Liftelo app; Stripe applies its own cookie rules.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or marketing tools, we will ask for your consent before loading them and update this page.
