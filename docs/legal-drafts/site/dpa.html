Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Version: draft of 2026-11-06 · Effective: [[DATUM_SNAGA: effective date]]

This annex to the Terms of Service governs the processing of personal data that a customer company enters into Liftelo. The customer is the controller, Liftelo is the processor.

How it is accepted: online acceptance in the app (with a record of version and time) is planned. Until then the DPA is accepted by signature or written confirmation on request.

1. Parties

Controller: the company using Liftelo (Customer). Processor: [[TVRTKA: full legal name]] ([[OBLIK: legal form]]), [[ADRESA: registered address]], OIB [[OIB: OIB (tax ID)]]. Privacy contact: [[EMAIL_PRIVATNOST: privacy e-mail]].

2. Subject matter, duration, nature of processing

Subject: providing the Liftelo app under the Terms of Service. Duration: for the contract term and the export and deletion period in section 10. Processing: storage, display, search, producing PDF reports and exports, sending notifications and e-mail, making backups.

3. Types of data and data subjects

  • Data subjects: the Customer's users (admins, technicians, viewers), the Customer's contact persons and clients (e.g. people who sign reports; residents or building managers if the Customer enters them).
  • Data: name, e-mail, role, language, sign-in identity (Google/Apple), password (hash), building addresses, contact details the Customer enters, technician names in service records, notes, photos, handwritten signatures (image), IP address when signing and in the action log.
  • The service is not intended for special categories of data; the Customer must not enter them.

4. Instructions and purpose

We process data only on the Customer's documented instructions: the Terms of Service, this DPA and the users' actions in the app. If we believe an instruction breaches a regulation, we will tell the Customer.

5. Confidentiality

People with access to data are bound by confidentiality. Our staff's access to Customer data is limited to what is needed for operation and support; superadmin actions on companies are logged with a reason.

6. Security

We apply the technical and organisational measures described on the Security page, including: separation of data per company, role-based access, protected passwords, private files and an action log. We may improve the measures; we will not materially reduce them.

7. Subprocessors

The Customer gives general authorisation for the subprocessors on the Subprocessors page. We notify of a new or replaced subprocessor at least [[ROK_OBAVIJEST: notice period for new subprocessor]] in advance; the Customer may object on reasonable grounds and, if we cannot agree, may terminate the service. We bind subprocessors by contract to appropriate data protection obligations.

8. Data subject rights and assistance

We help the Customer respond to data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), within the capabilities of the app. A request that reaches us directly is forwarded to the Customer without undue delay.

Service records are an unchangeable history. We fulfil a request to erase an individual by anonymising their personal data while keeping the record, unless the Customer and the law require otherwise. Until the in-app tools are finished we do this manually on written request from the administrator.

9. Personal data breaches

We notify the Customer of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's data without undue delay after becoming aware, with the information we have, so the Customer can meet its deadlines to the supervisory authority.

10. Termination: export and deletion

After the contract ends the Customer has [[ROK_IZVOZ: days to export after termination]] days to export its data. After that we delete the Customer's data from the app, unless the law requires retention. Backups disappear by rotation within [[ROK_BACKUP: backup deletion period]]. Deleting an entire company is a deliberate, confirmed procedure, never automatic. The tool for a full export is under development; until then we prepare the export on request.

11. Audits

On reasonable request we provide the information needed to demonstrate compliance. On-site audits are agreed in advance, during working hours, and must not endanger other customers' data.

12. Transfers outside the EEA

Hosting: [[IAAS: IaaS provider under Cloudways]], [[REGIJA: server region/country]]. Some subprocessors may process data outside the EEA; safeguard: [[PRIJENOSI: transfer mechanism outside the EEA]].

13. Liability and law

Liability is governed by the Terms of Service. Governing law and jurisdiction: [[MJERODAVNO_PRAVO: governing law]]; [[SUD_NADLEZAN: competent court]].
